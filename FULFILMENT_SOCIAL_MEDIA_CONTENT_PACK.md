# OneHub Social Media Content Pack — Fulfilment Specification

Status: custom service workflow specification. This file is not a pre-made customer deliverable and is not evidence of payment or a completed order.

## Commercial scope
- Price: US$30.54 one-time.
- Deliverables: 5 custom branded social post designs.
- Copy: 5 matching captions, one per design.
- Revisions: 1 consolidated revision round across the delivered set.
- Licence: single-business end use.
- Excluded: posting, ad spend, follower growth, sales guarantees, ongoing social-media management, and any separate 300-framework/template bundle.

## Preconditions before work begins
1. A genuine customer order exists.
2. Payment for this exact scope is independently verified.
3. A complete brief is available: business name, audience, offer/service, desired message, brand details, supplied assets/logos where applicable, and any relevant platform/context.
4. The order and payment can be linked to the same customer/transaction.
5. Bulk seats or multiple requested variants under one paid transaction still count as one commercial sale for sales reporting.

## Production checklist
For each of the 5 posts:
- Create an original branded design based on the customer's brief and supplied assets.
- Create one matching caption.
- Check spelling, factual claims, contact details supplied by customer, and brand consistency.
- Do not introduce fabricated testimonials, results, urgency, endorsements, traffic, followers or sales claims.
- Do not use unlicensed customer/third-party assets.

## Pre-delivery QA
- Exactly 5 final designs are present.
- Exactly 5 matching captions are present.
- Files open correctly and are clearly named.
- Customer-supplied names/contact details have been checked against the brief.
- Deliverables match the paid scope.
- No unrelated planned OneHub catalogue products are included or represented as purchased.

## Revision
- Collect requested changes as one consolidated revision round.
- Apply only changes within the agreed five-design/five-caption scope.
- Further work outside that scope requires a separate explicit agreement; do not imply it is included.

## Evidence required for a fulfilled order
Retain/order-link:
- verified paid transaction identifier/status;
- customer/order identifier;
- completed brief;
- final five designs;
- final five captions;
- revision record if used;
- delivery timestamp/evidence.

A website, enquiry, brief, directory submission, lead, click, or licence-seat count is never evidence of a paid sale.
